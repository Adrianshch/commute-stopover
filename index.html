Commute Stopover

Find errands between Woodford and Holborn on the Central line, ranked by how much time each one adds to your trip.

    How the numbers work. Time on the train is the same wherever you get off, so the extra time is just your detour. On the line, that's the walk there and back (straight-line distance × 1.3 at 4.8 km/h), plus the mid-trip cost of exiting, re-entering and waiting for the next train. That cost is skipped at Holborn and Woodford, since you start or finish there anyway. Off the line, it's the TfL Journey Planner round trip from the best Central line station, plus the same mid-trip cost.
